Settings

This app

The Android app's settings page has everything of this phone: which Retinue it shows and its endpoints, voice and shelf, health data, wake phrase, launcher bar and updates, followed by this instance's settings.

Phone shell

The Android app around this dashboard. Which Retinue it shows, and the phone's own settings: endpoints, launcher bar, wake phrase, updates.

Health data from the phone's Health Connect (watch, ring, blood-pressure app) is sent to your health records when the app comes to the front, at most once per interval below. An update started here always runs.

Health data is not uploaded to this Retinue. The switch is on the endpoint, in the phone shell.

This browser

Notifications

Web Push to this device: Ara notifies you when a conversation needs a decision or an answer arrives while the dashboard is closed. Per device — enable it on each phone or computer you want notified.

Voice

Continuous conversation (the loop button in a thread's bar) reads Ara's answers aloud, then opens the mic. A pause as long as the first value ends what you say and sends it; if you say nothing, the mic closes after the second (tap the mic to talk again). Per device.

Layout

On a wide screen the dashboard's columns are resizable — drag the dividers, double-click one to reset it. Each card's header switches between list and card view. Both are remembered per device.

Shelf

Threads, chats and projects you minimize — or leave while Ara is still answering — wait on the shelf at the foot of every page. When it is full, the item used longest ago with nothing new on it makes room; items with an answer or messages waiting are never dropped. Per device, shared by this browser's windows and tabs.
